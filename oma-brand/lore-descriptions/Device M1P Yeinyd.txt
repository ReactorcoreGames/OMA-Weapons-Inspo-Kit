NAME:		Device M1P Yeinyd

--

TYPE: 		Small tactical anti-personnel mine with many possible detection electronics and sensors within. Contains a compact but potent explosive charge within.

EFFECT: 	Can be used to create deadly explosive traps against human sized enemies. Can be triggered by laser, proximity, motion, timer, vibration or impact.

INTENT:		Versatile tool for soldiers, assassins and intruders alike to take down pursuing targets, create distractions or lay ambushes. Mercenaries usually just throw them like regular grenades at enemies. Works.
