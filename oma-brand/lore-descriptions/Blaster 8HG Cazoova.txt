NAME:		Blaster 8HG Cazoova

--

TYPE: 		High-tech two-handed particle rifle with a rectangular frontal barrel, an extendable stock, a smart targetting sensor unit on top, a removable power cell at the bottom near the pistol grip. The inside of the barrel is a mostly hollow corridor with a firing aperture deep inside, while the corridor is lined with EM field emitters that both accelerate and alter the trajectory of the projectiles fired by the weapon.

EFFECT: 	Tactical variable mode particle combat rifle that can alter how it fires and what it fires. In essence, the weapon can be made to behave like an assault rifle, shotgun, sniper rifle, a cannon, flamethrower or mix of any of these. The weapon draws power from its power cell into the ammo priming chamber where it will create a pure energy projectile by arranging, merging or splitting particles with charged EM fields. The projectile can be made into a single solid unit, multiple pellets grouped together. It can also be made more dense or accumulate into a larger projectile or do both. Depending on the parameters of the desired projectile, it will take more or less time between each projectile preparation, adjusting the weapon's rate of fire as a result - so cannon shots don't shoot as often as machine gun rounds. After the shot is prepared it is ejected with great speed out of the firing aperture. Now the shot is located at the hollow corridor with the EM field array system which can be set to adjust the how the shot will fly out of the gun. The EM field can be made into a cone which will cause the shot(s) to fly out in a spread. It can also be vectored and make the shots curve in a particular direction, allowing for shooting over and beside obstacle. The field may also add additional acceleration to the shot thus increasing its damage and range. The intensity of the EM field also affects the rate of fire, so the user needs to keep this mind as well. Using either the weapons own controls via top scope, buttons hidden under a dust cover or use a wireless link via helmet, implant or other external controller, the user may switch the weapon modes on the fly in less than a second, especially if they've prepared their favorite custom presets beforehand. The weapon is overall powerful, versatile, has a high ammo capacity (which scales with what mode is being used, with power-intensive modes drawing more power per shot, obviously) and can hit targets even at long ranges thanks to its smart sensor scope.

INTENT:		In the hands of a skilled operator this weapon's operation on the battlefield may appear beautiful how a single gun can instantly adapt itself to kill a target with the appropriate mode seamlessly. The sheer variety and amount of features of this high-end rifle are dazzling in terms of what kinds of attack styles are possible with it, making it far more advanced than any other infantry weapon out there. It can be equally by used against groups of enemies as well as against heavily armored vehicles. It can be made to penetrate through cover or have the shots explode on impact. In essence, its the ultimate all-in-one infantry weapon.
