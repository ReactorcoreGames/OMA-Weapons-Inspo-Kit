NAME:		Cannon WWW Jiofrax

--

TYPE: 		A massive high-tech nuclear railcannon with many large components that power and manage its operation. Its main barrel is lined with acceleration coils that can speed up a round to high-hypersonic velocities in conjuction with its main firing chamber that consist of a particle accelerator in itself. The giant telescope-sensor system can see targets beyond visual range at extreme distances and the holographic screen at its other end displays relevant information that helps the operator target the weapon and know of its status. The enourmous mega capacitor at the back allows for a tremendously powerful shot to be charged up during the weapon's firing sequence. Large heat vents are placed under the gun near its main reactor to deal with the high temperatures the gun has to withstand during full operation.

EFFECT: 	Extreme-range high-hypersonic fusion particle accelerator super cannon. The impact point of whatever hard surface this weapon hits will create an intense multi-kiloton thermonuclear explosion. Needless to say, this weapon is absolutely cataclysmic and requires a long distance to use safely. Thankfully the weapon has excellent safety features and operator guidance to ensure the weapon is used appropriately and accurately. Upon activation, the operator will be engulfed in a holographic view of what they're currently aiming at, if the trajectory is certain and path of flight unobstructed - showing it all with detailed, yet relevant 3D holo user interface elements. Once the operator locks on to a target they want to hit, the weapon will begin its lenghty firing sequence where it gradually charges up its capacitor for the fusion superbolt that is slowly fed into the particle accelerator in the middle of the gun. Once enough power and speed has been built-up, the weapon will make a final moment assessment if the line of fire is clean enough and if it is, the payload is rapidly condenced into a tight singularity and fired through the acceleration coils for a final speed boost. The tiny launched projectile will then rip through the air and pierce through all soft-enough materials like they're not even there, causing the mere trail of the railcannon shot to immolate and shatter them after a split second delay. Once the shot encounters something hard enough to impact against fully, it will then experience a magnetic field failure and unleash its destructive nuclear blast as a shaped charge. Pretty much everything caught in the blast will be eradicated as a result.

INTENT:		Originally designed as a portable orbital bombardment superweapon shot against terrestrial targets from space or high orbit, but it didn't stop some mercenaries bringing these guns to the ground and using them as apocalyptic siege cannons that could end a battle with a single shot. Conversely, it can be used as an orbital defence weapon when shot from the ground against targets in high-orbit or space.
