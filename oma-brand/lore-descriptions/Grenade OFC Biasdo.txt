NAME:		Grenade OFC Biasdo

--

TYPE: 		Cylindrical canister grenade with a stick handle for assisted throwing power.

EFFECT: 	A modern HE (High Explosive) grenade that can be thrown further thanks to its stick handle which provides extra leverage. Exceptionally lightweight yet reliable in its construction.

INTENT:		Common military equipment for ground soldiers on open battlefields against groups of armored enemies or entrenched positions.
