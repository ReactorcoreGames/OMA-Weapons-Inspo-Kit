NAME:		Sniper F5 Foxclore

--

TYPE: 		Large-caliber sniper rifle with a massive scope pre-built into the frame. Magazine fed, has a stock and a pistol grip.

EFFECT: 	Shoots a big lethal bullet at high speeds that travels very far - a quintessential sniper rifle. The large super scope provides extreme zoom, nightvision mode and target highlighting.

INTENT:		Standard military sharpshooter weapon assigned to reconnaissance teams to find, track and kill targets (up to medium armor), shooting from hidden positions at long distances.
