NAME:		Tool 3KY Oleald

--

TYPE: 		Modern electronics powered binocular unit. Uses specialized cameras and lenses to capture and project a video feed to the viewer via digital screen.

EFFECT: 	Comes with multiple functions to enhance the user's ability to see and detect things that normal human eyes can't - thermal vision, zoom magnification, night vision as well as EM spectrum vision for viewing UV light and radiation. Operates on battery power.

INTENT:		Developed for military use originally, but soon found itself commercially available to all industry/civilian applications too.
