NAME:		Grenade QM7 Parede

--

TYPE: 		Cylindrical fluid container grenade with a pressure dispersal nozzle.

EFFECT: 	A dastardly area denial weapon that sprays a mixture of liquid acid mist and poison gas, creating a lingering noxious cloud and dealing caustic damage to the surrounding area making it untraversable for any organic being for an extended time.

INTENT:		Used to stop swarms of small monsters from flooding through a tunnel passage and kill them in groups.
